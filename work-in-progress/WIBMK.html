السيرة النبوية لمحمد بن اسحاق في رواية ابن المبارك ابو عيد الرحمن عبد الله بن مبارك



TBJBV04P138B

al-Ṭabarī, Jāmiʿ al-bayān ʿan tāʾwīl āy al-Qurʾān, vol. 4 p. 138

حدثني المثنى قال حدثنا سويد قال أخبرنا @TR1_IBMK@ ابن المبارك عن محمد بن إسحاق عن نافع عن ابن عمر قال الفسوق ما أصيب من معاصي الله به صيد أو غيره.

COMMENT

This passage is stated in reference to Qurʾān 2:197.

FMSXV01P344B

Ibn ʿAbd al-Ḥakam al-Miṣrī, Futūḥ Miṣr waʾl-Maghrib, vol. 1 p. 344

ولهم عنه حديث واحد وهو ابن المبارك قال حدثنا محمد بن إسحاق عن يزيد ابن أبى حبيب عن مرثد بن عبد الله اليزنى عن مالك بن هبيرة أنه كان إذا شهد جنازة فتقال أهلها جزأهم ثلاثة صفوف ثم يقول قال رسول الله ما من مسلم يصلى عليه ثلاثة صفوف من المسلمين إلا أوجب.

COMMENT

This passage refers to the practice of Mālik b. Hubayrah, and his division of rows during funeral prayer. He is not mentioned in the redaction of Ibn Hishām. According to Ibn ʿAbd al-Ḥakam al-Miṣrī (d. 257/870), this is the only tradition attributed to Ibn Hubayrah (see, FMSXV01P344B (Ibn ʿAbd al-Ḥakam al-Miṣrī, Futūḥ Miṣr waʾl-Maghrib, vol. 1 p. 344)). For reasons that are unclear, it is also one of the most widely circulated traditions in the Ibn Isḥāq corpus, both in terms of the number of witnesses, but in the distribution across the permodern literary tradtion. He was Mālik b. Hubayrah b. Khālid b. Muslim b. al-Ḥārith b. Bakr b. Thaʿlabah b. ʿAqabah b. sl-Sakrān, he was apparently a companion, although little is known about how or when you joined the community. He was sometimes listed as Mālik b. Hubayrah al-Sakūnī (see, for instance, QNMXV03P043A (Ibn Qāniʿ, Muʿjam al-ṣaḥābah, vol. 3 p. 43)) and was close to Muʿāwiyah b. Abī Sifyān, who trusted his opinion and commanded armies for him. He died during the brief reign of Marwān (r. June 684-May 685), (see DKHXV05P289A (Aḥmad b. ʿAmr b.
